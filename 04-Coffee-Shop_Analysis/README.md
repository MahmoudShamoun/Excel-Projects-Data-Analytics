## Project Overview
This is an end-to-end **Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard** built using Microsoft Excel.  
It focuses on analyzing coffee shop sales transactions, identifying peak sales periods, top-performing products, and providing actionable insights through a clean, interactive dashboard.

## Objectives
- Analyze revenue trends by month, product category, and top products  
- Identify peak days and hours for operational optimization  
- Highlight top 15 product types driving the most transactions  
- Transform raw sales data into actionable business insights  

## Dashboard Insights
The dashboard provides insights into:  
- Overall revenue, total transactions, and total quantity sold  
- Revenue trends by month  
- Sales patterns by day of the week and hour  
- Product category and top 15 product performance  
- Peak hours and days for operational decision-making  

**Note on Colors:**  
The dashboard uses a **clean and professional color palette**, not the typical coffee-themed colors, to ensure clarity, readability, and better visualization of data trends.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel  
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts  
- Slicers  
- Dynamic Excel formulas  
- Dashboard design & data storytelling  

## Dataset Description
The dataset includes sales transaction-level information such as:  
- Transaction ID, date, and time  
- Product details (category, type, detail)  
- Quantity and unit price  
- Store location
