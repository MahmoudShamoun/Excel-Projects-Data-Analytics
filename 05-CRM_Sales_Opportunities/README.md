## Project Overview
This is an end-to-end **CRM Sales Dashboard** built using Microsoft Excel as the.  
The dashboard provides a high-level view of sales operations, helping stakeholders track net profit, monitor the sales pipeline, and evaluate the performance of managers and agents across different regions.

## Objectives
- **Analyze Profitability:** Visualize monthly profit fluctuations (reaching up to $1.3M in peak months).  
- **Sales Pipeline Management:** Track the status of 8,800+ total opportunities from "Prospecting" to "Won".  
- **Performance Benchmarking:** Identify top-performing sales managers (e.g., Melvin Marxen) and agents.  
- **Regional Analysis:** Provide interactive filtering for Central, East, and West regions.  
- **Outcome Optimization:** Compare Win vs. Lost percentages across quarters to identify seasonal trends.  

## Dashboard Insights
The dashboard provides insights into:  
- **Net Profit:** Totaled $10,005,534 with detailed monthly breakdowns.  
- **Efficiency:** Q1 showed the highest "Won" percentage at 82.07%.  
- **Human Capital:** Top managers are ranked by the volume of successful deals handled.  
- **Opportunity Tracking:** Monitoring total opportunities (8,800) vs. Won (4,238) and Lost (2,473) cases.  
- **Leaderboard:** Highlighting Top-5 Managers and Sales Agents based on successful conversions.  
- **Regional & Quarterly Performance:** Dynamic filtering by Region (Central, East, West) and Quarters (Q1-Q4).  
- **Win/Loss Ratio:** Analyzing quarterly outcomes to optimize sales strategies.  

## Tools & Technologies
